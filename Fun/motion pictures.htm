Motion Pictures

Over the years I've done projects that lean into my 3d & 2d motion graphics background for fun and for work as a creative outlet 🎨. Main tools of choice are After Effects, Photoshop, and Maya.

Looping Animation Vignettes

Illustrated and animated a set of three vignettes, inspired by Google Doodles and their use of the logo as a self contained storytelling space. 3D was used for moving objects and characters for freedom of animation, and 2D illustration to set the visual style and tone.
